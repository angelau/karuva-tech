Welcome to Pilex Centre For Civic Education Initiative

Pilex Centre for Civic Education Initiative is a public interest law Centre focused on Democracy and Good Governance, Human and Environmental rights defence through strategic litigation and advocacy. With six full-time staff, Pilex Centre has an out-sized impact through its partnerships with leading public interest lawyers and legal advocacy organizations in Africa, America and the U.K.

What we work on

Three areas where we use the law, evidence and public advocacy to protect people.

Democracy and good governance

We push for accountable government and informed citizens, so people can take part in the decisions that affect their lives.

Human rights

We stand with people whose rights have been denied or ignored, through legal representation and public advocacy.

Environment and climate change

We challenge pollution, oil spills and land damage, backed by scientific evidence and field research.

We work with leading public interest lawyers and legal advocacy organizations in Africa, America and the U.K.

The people behind the work

Lawyers, scientists and media professionals working together for communities across the Niger Delta.
