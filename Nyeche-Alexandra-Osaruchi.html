Pilex Centre First 2026 Education Grant Beneficiary

April 20, 2026 By Communications Officer

At PILEX Centre for Civic Education Initiative, we believe that education is the most powerful tool for civic transformation and sustainable development. Today, we are proud to turn that belief into action.

The Storyline

Nyeche Alexandra Osaruchi is the very first beneficiary of the PILEX Centre Education Grant for 2026.

This grant isn't just a certificate or a financial contribution; it is an investment in a bright mind and a commitment to ensuring that financial barriers do not stand in the way of academic excellence and community leadership.

As we navigate the challenges of 2026, the need for educated, civic-minded leaders has never been greater. By supporting students like Alexandra, we are strengthening the foundation of our society.
