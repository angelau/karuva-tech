About Our Organization

Pilex Centre For Civic Education Initiative is a public interest law centre based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. We defend human and environmental rights through strategic litigation and advocacy.

July 2023 Pilex Centre registered
160 Cases of attacks on rights defenders recorded in Kaduna, 2022 to 2023
2024 Secretariat secured in Port Harcourt

Who We Are

Our story so far.

  1. July 2023

    Pilex Centre is registered

    Pilex Centre was registered in July 2023 and within a twinkling of an eye, we are everywhere, tirelessly working to make life better for humanity.

  2. September 2023

    Documenting attacks on rights defenders in Kaduna

    In September 2023, we went into Kaduna, Nigeria to profile and verify Human and Environmental Rights Defenders who had suffered reprisal attacks from 2022 to 2023. We recorded 160 cases of such incidents that led to the death or injury of affected defenders. Global reporters of such incidents have not been capturing Nigeria and Africa in their yearly reports.

    Hence the support from Green Advocates/Mano River Union to undertake the task. We completed the project on September 28, 2023 and produced a report which we are yet to publish.

  3. 2024

    Secretariat, Legal Trainee program and RIV 3 Project

    Pushing forward into 2024, we secured our international Pilex Secretariat at 3b Peremabiri Street, D-Line, Port Harcourt, Nigeria and hit the ground running with our Legal Trainee program and RIV 3 Project (focused on Environmental Impact Assessment and litigation in the oil and gas extractive sector), sponsored by Advocates for Community Alternative (ACA) and Public Interest Lawyering Initiative for West Africa (PILIWA).

Our Mission

To stand up for the rights of Individuals, Communities and Organization in view of ensuring justice for all through the use of Legal Authorities and Advocacy.

Our Vision

To stand out as the Major Voice Of The People and Leading Public Interest Litigation/Advocacy Hub Globally.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about who we are and how we work.

What is Pilex Centre For Civic Education Initiative?

Pilex Centre For Civic Education Initiative, also known as Pilex Centre, is a public interest law centre. It works on democracy and good governance and defends human and environmental rights through strategic litigation and advocacy.

Where is Pilex Centre based?

Pilex Centre is based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria, and works with communities in Rivers State and across the Niger Delta.

When was Pilex Centre founded?

Pilex Centre was registered in July 2023. In 2024 it secured its secretariat in Port Harcourt.

What does Pilex Centre work on?

Its work covers democracy and good governance, human rights, and environment and climate change, including pollution, oil spills and land damage. It uses legal representation, strategic litigation, scientific evidence, field research and advocacy.

Who does Pilex Centre work with?

Pilex Centre partners with public interest lawyers and legal advocacy organizations in Africa, America and the U.K. Its projects have been supported by Green Advocates/Mano River Union, Advocates for Community Alternative (ACA) and the Public Interest Lawyering Initiative for West Africa (PILIWA).
