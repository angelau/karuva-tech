Meet Our Leadership

The passionate individuals driving our mission forward.

Board of Directors

Everest Nwankwo

Board Member

Everest holds a master degree in Environmental Law from University of Dundee, Scotland. He has great passion for development and environmental issues.

Our Dedicated Staff

Courage Nsirimovu

Coordinator

Courage Nsirimovu is the coordinator of Pilex Centre For Civic Education Initiative. He is a Public Interest Advocate and Litigation Lawyer who works on matters relating to Democracy and Good Governance, Human Rights, Environment and Climate Change. Courage is a member of Public Interest Lawyering Initiative for West Africa, He is also a member of NBA Port Harcourt Human Rights Committee. His passion is to speak out and take actions for the good of people globally.

Timizimor Victory

Programmes Officer

Meet Victory, our Programmes Officer! She's passionate about learning and adapting to new challenges, opportunities, and fostering a positive work environment where team members feel valued and motivated. She's focused on delivering high-quality results.

Peace Hachikaru Amadi

Media Team & Website Developer

Peace is the developer behind the Pilex Centre website and a key member of the media team. She designs and builds the Centre’s digital presence, and she pairs that technical skill with photography, video, graphics, and social media content. The result is a brand that looks professional and tells the Centre’s story clearly everywhere it appears online.

Chinualum N. Mmuozoba

In-House Counsel/Compliance Officer

Chinualum is our In-House Counsel and Compliance Officer, responsible for providing legal advice and ensuring compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

Favour Anichebe

Legal Counsel, Human Rights

Favour Anichebe is a human rights lawyer at Pilex Centre, where she stands for people whose rights have been denied or ignored. Through legal representation and advocacy, she works to secure justice and accountability for the vulnerable.

Mercy Ndubuisi

Environmental Scientist & Advocate

Mercy is an environmental scientist and advocate who serves as a technical expert supporting the Pilex Centre legal team. She provides scientific evidence and analysis for cases involving pollution, oil spills, and land damage, reviews environmental impact assessments, and conducts field research and investigations for litigation.

King Obulor

Media Team Member | Law Student

King Obulor is a law student and member of the Pilex Centre media team. He joined the Centre as an intern and grew into the media team, where he edits videos, creates content, and posts across the Centre’s social media pages.
